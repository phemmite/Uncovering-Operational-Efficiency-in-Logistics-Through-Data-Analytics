🧭 Project Title:
Uncovering Operational Efficiency in Logistics Through Data Analytics

🧩 Project Summary
This project explores a real logistics dataset containing thousands of order transactions across multiple suppliers, warehouses, and customers in Nigeria.
Using Excel, Power Query, and PivotTables, I cleaned, transformed, and analyzed the data to uncover insights about sales performance, profitability, inventory movement, and delivery efficiency.

The goal was to turn raw logistics data into actionable insights that help businesses make smarter decisions about cost, delivery, and customer management.

🔍 Key Insights
1. Customer Accounts & Revenue
Apex Global Enterprises led total sales, contributing over ₦6 billion.

Client Business 28 Ltd and 32 Ltd also ranked among top performers.

Apex Global Enterprise 33 and 17 had the highest overdue balances, each exceeding ₦2.3 billion — a clear signal for tighter credit control.
Recommendation: Implement automated credit alerts and payment tracking dashboards to reduce outstanding balances.

2. Product Profitability
Packaging Materials and Office Supplies delivered the strongest margins (≈ 37 %), while Construction Materials lagged behind at ≈ 25 %.

High‑volume items like Portland Cement 50 kg Bag and Steel Rebar 12 mm (Ton) showed lower profitability despite strong demand.
Recommendation: Re‑evaluate supplier pricing and logistics costs for construction items to improve margin efficiency.

3. Inventory Movement
Fast‑moving products: A4 Copy Paper Box, Bubble Wrap Roll, Corrugated Boxes, and Stretch Wrap Film — each above 30 000 units ordered.

Slow‑moving products: Industrial Packaging Strapping Machine and Rubber Conveyor Belt 10 m — potential overstock.
Recommendation: Balance inventory by restocking high‑demand items and reducing low‑turnover stock to free warehouse space.

4. Delivery Performance
Most deliveries completed within 3–7 days, but Industrial Equipment and Construction Materials had frequent delays.

Longer delivery times correlated with lower profit margins — suggesting hidden logistics costs.
Recommendation: Optimize carrier selection and route planning using historical delay data to improve turnaround time.

5. Operational Efficiency
Profitability and delivery reliability are closely linked.

Customers with consistent on‑time deliveries also showed higher repeat orders and payment compliance.
Recommendation: Build a performance dashboard tracking on‑time delivery rate, average profit margin, and customer risk score in real time.

⚙️ Tools & Techniques
Excel Power Query: Data cleaning, transformation, and filtering.

PivotTables & Charts: KPI aggregation and visualization.

Formulas Used:

Profit Margin → =(Unit_Selling_Price - Unit_Cost)/Unit_Selling_Price

Outstanding Amount → =Sales_Amount - Payment_Amount

Delivery Days → =Actual_Delivery_Date - Order_Date

Fast/Slow Movers → PivotTable sorting by Sum of Quantity_Ordered.

🧭 Recommendations 
Strengthen Credit Control:  
Introduce automated alerts for overdue accounts and enforce stricter payment terms for high‑risk customers.

Optimize Pricing Strategy:  
Re‑evaluate supplier contracts and pricing for low‑margin categories like Construction Materials to improve profitability.

Inventory Rationalization:  
Reduce slow‑moving stock and prioritize restocking of high‑demand items to improve cash flow and warehouse utilization.

Enhance Delivery Efficiency:  
Use historical delay data to optimize carrier selection and route planning, reducing delivery time and hidden costs.

Performance Dashboards:  
Develop a Power BI dashboard that tracks KPIs such as on‑time delivery rate, profit margin, and customer risk score for real‑time decision‑making.

Predictive Analytics:  
Apply forecasting models to anticipate demand fluctuations and potential delivery delays before they impact operations.

🧩 Conclusion
This analysis demonstrates how data can transform logistics operations from reactive to proactive.
By integrating these insights into daily decision‑making, management can:

Improve profitability through smarter pricing and cost control.

Strengthen customer relationships by reducing overdue balances.

Enhance operational agility with faster deliveries and optimized inventory.

Ultimately, this project proves that data isn’t just about numbers — it’s about clarity, control, and confidence in every business decision.
